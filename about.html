ABOUT US • ERNAKULAM, KOCHI

One crate of tomatoes
at Broadway, 1998.

Silsa started as a 200 sq ft corner shop near Broadway, Ernakulam. Today it's Marine Drive's 45,000 sq ft universe — but the rule never changed: if we wouldn't serve it at our own sadya, it doesn't go on your shelf.

🌱

Farm First, Kerala First

60+ Kerala farms — Alappuzha veggies, Wayanad spices, Vazhakulam pineapples — plus day-boat fish from Fort Kochi harbour, every dawn.

🔥

Baked at 4AM

Puttu, appam batter, banana chips in coconut oil by 7:30AM — plus 60+ breads & cakes, zero waste.

⚡

Try Before You Love

People, Always

THE JOURNEY

How a Broadway stall ate Kochi.

1998

🧺 Silsa opens as a tiny veg stall near Broadway, Ernakulam. One crate, one dream, dangerously good nadan tomatoes.

2005

🥖 The bakery is born — pazhampori, banana chips & the 7:30AM puttu line that still stretches down the street.

2013

🏬 Silsa moves to the 45,000 sq ft Marine Drive flagship facing the backwaters. Kochi shows up.

2019

⚡ Floor 2 Home Lab launches — Kochi's first live-try appliance arena, monsoon-tested.

2026

🌟 12,000+ products, 60+ Kerala farms, harbour-fresh fish daily, 1M+ happy faces a year. Namaskaram, and we're just warming up.
