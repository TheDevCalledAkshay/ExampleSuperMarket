OUR STORY

Started as a Broadway corner shop. Now Kochi's living room.

Silsa began in 1998 near Broadway, Ernakulam with one crate of tomatoes and a stubborn belief: shopping should make you smile. 27 years later — farmers who are family, bakers who never sleep, and customers who come for milk and stay for lunch.
